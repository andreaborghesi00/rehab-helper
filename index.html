    Protocol rules
    • Bridge: a step unlocks after 2 painless sessions and at least 3 days at that load. Pain is the gate, not the calendar.
    • +1 kg below 25 kg, +2 kg above — keeping the relative step roughly constant.
    • Test your max only once the bridge is done and painless. One hand, on the edge you trained on.
    • Block: 10 s on / 50 s off, 2× a day, at least 6 h apart, for 30 days.
    • Working loads are % of your tested max, not % of bodyweight.
    • Never change edge depth and load in the same step. Drop the edge, drop the weight, rebuild.
    • Two hands share the load: a two-hand hang at T kg total is T/2 per hand.

    Physio rules
    • 20 mm edge, half crimp. Start at 10% bodyweight, build to 30%.
    • Never both exercises in one session, and always ≥ 6 h apart.
    • Alternate static → dynamic → static → …
    • Skip if you're climbing that day (max 1 h, with H-taping).
    • Add ~1 kg every 4 sessions.
    • At 30% BW you can climb longer than 1 h. Keep going 1 more month; if painless, drop the H-tape. If pain remains, step back.
